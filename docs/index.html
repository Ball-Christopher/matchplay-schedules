Matchplay schedules

Balanced schedules for 3-way and 4-way matchplay leagues of 11 to 40 bowlers, plus hybrid seasons that switch between 2-, 3- and 4-way so every couple meets exactly once. In every schedule each couple of bowlers meets equally often, and every bowler gets an even share of the lane pairs and of the bowling positions. Pick a league size, add names if you like, and export the result.
