# Searches and remaining questions for leagues up to 40

| Question | What the search established | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| 24 bowlers in fours, one-round prefix spread | The published lane-exact schedule attains two. A spread-one round must begin with a seven-week packing whose missing pairs are six disjoint four-cycles. A search with a cyclic action on the six components and a fixed week gave no witness. | A spread-one schedule or an impossibility proof. The symmetry restriction and search limits leave other packings possible. |
| 24 bowlers in trios, two rounds | Recolourings of the oil round into three clash-free classes include classes as short as four weeks. Searches for a relabelling that fits a class across the fixed boundary did not close the gap. | Oil first is 5–6. Main first is 18–19. |
| 36 bowlers in fours, two rounds | The new four-pattern round supplies the second half of both witnesses. Its week-conflict graph has a unique partition into four nonempty colour classes up to renaming colours, with sizes 8, 9, 9, 9. The published oil round ends in four clash-free weeks, so it cannot absorb this eight-week class in full. | With the published first rounds, oil is 14–15 and main is 29–30. A different second-round design could attain either lower endpoint. |
| Five lane pairs, three bowlers per pair, six qualifying blocks | All seven resolved Kirkman systems on 15 bowlers and all 49 one-class deletions were tested. None of the deletions admits balanced lanes. Cyclic templates, involution searches, replacement of two weeks of a packing, and partial-draw repair also gave no one-meeting witness. | The published (2, 1) remains attainable. A general six-week packing can have a leave other than five triangles, so the classification does not exclude (1, 1). |
| Qualifying, other lengths | Fixing groups and changing bowling order improved several 11- and 12-block draws. Some published groupings still forced duplicate directed follows under the orderings tried. Reassigning lanes on a social-golfer packing also failed in several sizes. | A failed lane assignment applies only to that packing. The results table distinguishes achieved bounds from mathematical optima. |

The finite enumerations for the 24- and 32-bowler fixed boundaries and the Kirkman checks have standalone scripts in the archive. The search for small league schedules used the updated first-round files in commit 33d41db.

The uniform 36-player trios product yielded a lane-exact one-round schedule. Its short opening pattern did not embed as three disjoint copies across the fixed oil tail. Enumerating all four three-week types then produced a mixed-layer bridge. The internal completion and twelve-week boundary pass independent checks; the mixed schedule still needs a full lane and position assignment. The checkpoint and the next exact-cover formulation are in [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md).
