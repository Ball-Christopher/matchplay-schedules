Z_7 x 4 classes (point 7*class+i) plus 4 fixed bowlers; i->i+1 moves lane pairs 1-7 up one and fixes lane pair 8 (as v32). The design also has the order-3 symmetry alpha: (class c, i) -> (pi(c), 2i), pi = (0)(1 2 3), fixed bowlers permuted likewise (group Z_7 x| Z_3 of order 21). Weeks: base week W and its images alpha(W), alpha^2(W), plus an alpha-invariant base week W0, each developed mod 7 (28 weeks); 3 invariant weeks T, alpha(T), alpha^2(T) (the four fixed bowlers together plus a transversal group developed mod 7). Blocks: the 7 translates of alpha^k(W) with alpha^k(T) (8 weeks each, k = 0,1,2) and the 7 translates of W0. A block is clash-free because its base week uses no pair-orbit twice and none of its invariant week's. W0 sampled at random (alpha-invariant, 48 distinct pair-orbits); W by exact backtracking on pair-orbit capacities per alpha-orbit (ext40/fable32/dfs.c, f21.py; seconds to a minute); lanes by DFS on (lane - i) residues (lanes.py), exact. Blocks and weeks inside blocks ordered by annealing for prefix spread; positions by positions.assign. 4 blocks is the lower bound (a clash-free run is at most 10 weeks).
